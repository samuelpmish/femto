Coffee mug vibration modes

3D modal analysis of a procedurally generated mug.

Try it

open full screen ↗

Physics

The free vibration modes of an elastic body satisfy the generalized eigenproblem

\[ K\,\mathbf{x} = \omega^2 M\,\mathbf{x}, \]

with stiffness matrix $K$ and mass matrix $M$; the lowest few eigenpairs give the mug’s resonant frequencies $f = \omega/2\pi$ and mode shapes. SI units throughout.

Implementation in femto

The mug is generated procedurally. Both matrices are single integrate calls:

K = sparse_matrix<>(integrate(dot(grad(phi), C_q, grad(phi)), domain));
M = sparse_matrix<>(integrate(dot(phi, rho_q, phi), domain));

and the lowest modes come from a shift-inverted Lanczos iteration in the $M$-inner product (see mug_analysis.hpp).
