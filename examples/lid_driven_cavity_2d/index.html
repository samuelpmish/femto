Lid-driven cavity

2D incompressible Navier-Stokes with Taylor-Hood elements, Newton to the steady state.

Try it

open full screen ↗

Physics

A square box of fluid is driven by its top wall sliding at speed $U$; the other walls are no-slip. The steady incompressible Navier–Stokes equations are

\[ (\mathbf{u}\cdot\nabla)\mathbf{u} - \nu\Delta\mathbf{u} + \nabla p = 0, \qquad \nabla\cdot\mathbf{u}=0, \]

and the Reynolds number $\mathrm{Re} = UL/\nu$ sets the shape of the central vortex.

Implementation in femto

Taylor–Hood elements (Q2 velocity, Q1 pressure) with the steady equations solved directly by Newton’s method on the coupled system, rather than time-marching to a steady state. The first step from rest is the Stokes solution; $\mathrm{Re}$ is then raised in stages so every Newton solve starts close to its answer. The Jacobian blocks are each one integrate:

Kuu = integrate(dot(grad(phi), nu_q,    grad(phi)), domain);
Cuu = integrate(dot(grad(phi), conv_q,  phi),       domain);
Gup = integrate(dot(psi,       gradp_q, grad(phi)), domain);

They are scattered into one unsymmetric matrix over $[\mathbf{u};p]$, factorized with LU, and the factorization is update()d rather than rebuilt at each step.
