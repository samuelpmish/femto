femto physics examples

Each page runs one example from examples/cpp. They share one webassembly module and fetch whichever mesh they need, then draw it and the fields they solve for.

parallel-plate capacitor — 2D electrostatics, capacitance from the field energy

TO-220 regulator — 3D conduction from the silicon die out to the air, on a choice of heatsink

fracture specimen — 2D finite-deformation elasticity, a notch opened in load steps

coffee mug modes — 3D modal analysis of a procedurally generated mug

induction coil — 3D magnetostatics on a tetrahedral mesh of copper, ferrite and air

lid-driven cavity — 2D incompressible Navier-Stokes, Taylor-Hood, Newton to the steady state

pressure pulse in a disk — 2D acoustics against the analytic solution: hard wall, absorbing condition, or a PML ring
