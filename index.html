femto

femto is a small C++20 finite element library. Problems are written close to their weak forms: you create fields on a mesh, write an integrand with dot(...) over test and trial functions, and integrate(...) it over a domain to get a matrix or residual vector.

Field<Family::H1> V = create_field<Family::H1>(mesh, 1, 1);
BasisFunction phi(V);
sparse_matrix K = integrate(dot(grad(phi), eps_q, grad(phi)), domain);

What it provides

  • Meshes and elements — triangles, quads, tetrahedra and hexahedra, with H1 and H(curl) spaces of arbitrary polynomial degree.
  • Quadrature-point kernels — material laws are written as small functors invoked through forall(...), so the same code runs threaded on the CPU or on a GPU.
  • Sparse direct solvers — MKL or PaStiX on the CPU, cuDSS on the GPU.
  • WebAssembly — every example in this site is the same C++ source, compiled with emscripten and running in your browser.
